Privacy Policy

How Sea2Sky Traders collects, uses and protects your information.

At Sea2Sky Traders, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you browse our site, place an order, or contact our team. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number and shipping address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Order information: Products selected, quantities, order value and delivery preferences.
  • Communication content: Messages you send us via WhatsApp, email, the contact form or social media.
  • Technical information: Browser type, device information and general usage data collected automatically when you visit our site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including packing and shipping
  • Communicate with you about your order status, quotes and enquiries
  • Respond to customer support requests and resolve issues
  • Improve our products, website and customer experience
  • Send occasional updates about new products or offers, where you've agreed to receive them

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Shipping & courier partners: To deliver your order to the correct address.
  • Payment processors: Where applicable, to process transactions securely.
  • Legal authorities: If required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of our customers.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

WhatsApp & Communication

Much of our order confirmation, support and updates happen via WhatsApp. When you message us, we use your number solely to respond to your enquiry or order — it is not added to any third-party marketing list. Please note that message security is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms of service.

Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your shopping cart between pages, so your selections aren't lost as you browse. This information stays on your own device and is not transmitted to us until you choose to check out via WhatsApp. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or WhatsApp is 100% secure, and we encourage you to avoid sharing sensitive financial details such as full card numbers through chat.

Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, where legally possible
  • Opt out of promotional messages at any time by telling us on WhatsApp or email

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults placing personal or business orders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Questions About This Policy?

If you have any questions about how we handle your information, reach out to us directly — we're happy to help.
