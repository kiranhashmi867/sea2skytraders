Shipping policy

We ship every order from our Lahore, Pakistan facility to customers around the world. Here is how it works:

  • Processing time: Orders are prepared and packed within 1–2 business days after confirmation.
  • Delivery time: Varies by destination country and courier. We confirm exact timelines on WhatsApp once your order is placed.
  • Order confirmation: All orders are confirmed directly with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.
  • Bulk and wholesale orders: May require additional preparation time. Ask us for an estimate.
  • Tracking: A tracking number or shipment update is shared with you once your order is dispatched.

Returns and refunds

Customer satisfaction matters to us. If there is a problem with your order, here is what to do:

  • Report window: Contact us within 3 days of delivery for damaged, defective or incorrect items.
  • How to report: Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and a photo of the item received.
  • Resolution: We will offer a replacement, exchange or refund depending on the situation, once reviewed.
  • Perishable items: Dry fruits and fresh fruits can only be returned if received damaged or spoiled.
  • Return shipping: Covered by us if the issue is on our end; otherwise discussed case by case.

Shipping and returns FAQ

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, Sea2Sky Traders ships worldwide, including the USA, UK, Canada and across Europe, directly from our facility in Lahore, Pakistan.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Delivery time depends on your destination country. Domestic orders in Pakistan typically arrive in 2–4 business days, while international orders can take 7–18 business days. We will confirm an exact estimate on WhatsApp once your order is placed.

Can I track my order?

Yes, once your order is dispatched we share a tracking number or shipment update with you directly on WhatsApp.

What if my item arrives damaged or wrong?

Message us on WhatsApp within 3 days of delivery with your order details and a photo of the item. We will arrange a replacement, exchange or refund depending on the situation.

Can I return dry fruits or fresh fruits?

Since these are perishable items, returns are only accepted if the product arrives damaged or spoiled. Please report this within 3 days of delivery with photo proof.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale shipping?

Yes, we handle bulk and wholesale orders. These may require additional processing time. Message us on WhatsApp for a shipping and pricing estimate.

Have a question about your order?

Our team is one message away on WhatsApp for shipping updates, return requests or anything else.
